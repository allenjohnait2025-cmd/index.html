Typical AI security workflow

Collect data

Use authorised, relevant, privacy-aware data.

Prepare signals

Clean data and extract useful features.

Score & evaluate

Measure precision, recall, false alarms, and bias.

Review & respond

Human validation, documentation, and feedback.

Research questions

Detection quality

How do AI methods compare with rule-based baselines across representative datasets?

Explainability

Can a system provide clear, actionable reasons for each alert?

Responsible deployment

How can teams reduce privacy risk, bias, false positives, and adversarial evasion?

05 / Conclusion

AI assists defenders — people stay accountable.

AI has potential to improve speed and scale in cybersecurity, but robust deployment needs representative data, transparent evaluation, continuous monitoring, and human judgement. This website is a student demonstration of those concepts, not a production security product.
